# Coinpers 

Coinpers is a mobile application built with Flutter and Dart, designed to help individuals log daily expenses, categorize spending, and keep a clear, offline-first record of where their money goes.

## Core Features

- ➕ **Quick Expense Logging**: Add a title, amount, and category in seconds through a simple dialog.
- 🏷️ **Category Tagging**: Organize every expense under categories like Food, Transport, Bills, Shopping, and Others.
- 💵 **Real-Time Total**: See your running total spend update instantly at the top of the app.
- 🗑️ **Swipe to Delete**: Remove an expense with a single swipe  no extra taps needed.
- 📅 **Auto-Dated Entries**: Every expense is timestamped automatically for accurate tracking.
- 📴 **Fully Offline**: All data is stored locally on-device  no internet connection or account required.

## Workflow

1. **Open the App**: Launch Coinpers no sign-up, no login, just start tracking.
2. **Add an Expense**: Tap the `+` button, enter a title and amount, then pick a category.
3. **Track Your Spending**: Watch your total update live as you log each transaction.
4. **Review Your List**: Scroll through your expense history, sorted from most recent.
5. **Clean Up Entries**: Swipe left on any item to delete a mistaken or duplicate entry.
6. **Stay on Budget**: Check your running total anytime to stay aware of your spending habits.
